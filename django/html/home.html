What file formats does PrismESG support? +
PrismESG supports PDF, DOCX, and scanned documents via OCR. Most BRSR and ESG reports in India are published as PDFs, and we handle both text-native and scanned versions.
How accurate is the AI extraction? +
Our extraction model achieves 92% accuracy on standard ESG disclosures across GRI and BRSR frameworks, validated on 200+ Indian sustainability reports.
Is my report data kept confidential? +
Yes. Each workspace is fully isolated. Reports you upload are never used to train models and are not visible to other users.
Can I analyze reports for multiple companies? +
Yes. Professional and Enterprise plans support multiple company workspaces with isolated analysis and reporting.
