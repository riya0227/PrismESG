PrismESG

An AI platform for analyzing ESG disclosures with consistency, speed, and transparency.

Overview

ESG reporting has grown rapidly in scale and complexity, but the process of evaluating disclosures remains largely manual and inconsistent. PrismESG addresses this gap by applying structured analysis to sustainability reports using machine learning models.

The platform is designed to extract disclosures, classify them into standardized categories, and identify gaps where reporting lacks measurable evidence.

What the platform does

  • Extracts ESG disclosures from BRSR and sustainability reports
  • Classifies content into Policy, Action, and Metric layers
  • Identifies reporting gaps and weak disclosures
  • Evaluates imbalance across ESG pillars
  • Flags potential greenwashing risk

Approach

PrismESG uses a structured PAM (Policy–Action–Metric) framework to evaluate disclosures. The system prioritizes measurable evidence over narrative statements, allowing for a more objective assessment of ESG quality.

Outputs are standardized to ensure comparability across companies, sectors, and reporting periods.
