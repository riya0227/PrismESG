Project objective
Understanding ESG disclosures should not be complex or manual

ESG reports are long and unstructured, making it difficult to extract insights. This project uses AI to classify disclosures into Policy–Action–Metric categories.

The goal is to demonstrate how intelligent systems can support ESG analysis efficiently.

03
